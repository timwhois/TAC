Choosing between physical, AI and 3D

Each route has strengths. Physical photography captures real texture and light. AI production with Shootless scales on-model imagery quickly from real garments. 3D gives total control over geometry, materials and environment — ideal for hard goods, prototypes and spaces.

We help you choose the right mix for each brief, so budget goes where it has the most impact. For garments on models, Shootless is usually the faster route; for hard goods, packaging, prototypes and environments, CGI product rendering usually is.

How a CGI product rendering project works

Every render starts with references: photography of the product or prototype, dimensions, material and finish details, and CAD files where they exist. From those we build the 3D product model, then develop the materials so metal, plastic, glass, fabric or print read correctly under light.

Lighting and camera are approached the way a photographer would approach a product shoot — key light, reflections, depth of field, angle and crop — because the goal is a render that sits comfortably next to real photography. You review drafts, we refine, and final renders are delivered at the sizes and crops each channel needs. Once the model exists, new colourways, angles and settings come from the same file rather than a new shoot.

3D set design and environments

The same toolkit is used for spaces. A set can be modelled to real dimensions so a build is agreed before any materials are bought, and campaign or retail environments can be visualised for approval or used as finished CGI backgrounds. We work alongside experienced set builders when a concept moves from screen to studio, and our production management team can take the build from there.

Questions

What do you need to create a product render?

Reference photography, dimensions and material details, or existing CAD files where available.

Can you work from CAD files?

Yes. Existing CAD files are a good starting point for CGI product modelling; where none exist, we model from reference photography and dimensions.

How long does a CGI product render take?

It depends on the product's complexity, how much modelling is needed and how many views and variations you need. We will give you a timeline once we have seen your references.

Can 3D renders look photographic?

Yes. CGI can be produced to a photographic standard, or stylised to suit the brand.

Can you build 3D environments for websites?

Yes. We create 3D environments for campaigns, presentations and interactive digital experiences.

When is 3D better than a photoshoot?

When the product or set doesn't exist yet, when a build would be impractical, or when you need many controlled variations.

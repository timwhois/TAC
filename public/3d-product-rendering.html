Some images are better made than shot. A product that isn't manufactured yet, a set too large or expensive to construct, an environment that doesn't exist, or a range of variations that would take weeks to photograph — these are the moments where 3D earns its place.

Third Axis Creative produces CGI product renders and 3D environments as part of a wider production toolkit. Because we also run physical shoots and AI production, we use 3D where it is genuinely the best route rather than by default.

Choosing between physical, AI and 3D

Each route has strengths. Physical photography captures real texture and light. AI production with Shootless scales on-model imagery quickly from real garments. 3D gives total control over geometry, materials and environment — ideal for hard goods, prototypes and spaces.

We help you choose the right mix for each brief, so budget goes where it has the most impact.

Questions

What do you need to create a product render?

Reference photography, dimensions and material details, or existing CAD files where available.

Can 3D renders look photographic?

Yes. CGI can be produced to a photographic standard, or stylised to suit the brand.

Can you build 3D environments for websites?

Yes. We create 3D environments for campaigns, presentations and interactive digital experiences.

When is 3D better than a photoshoot?

When the product or set doesn't exist yet, when a build would be impractical, or when you need many controlled variations.

How do we start a 3D project?

Email hello@thirdaxis.co.uk with references and what the visuals are for.

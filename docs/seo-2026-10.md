# SEO changes — October 2026

Source: Google Search Console audit of `sc-domain:thirdaxis.co.uk`, data to 26 Oct 2026. Volumes are small, so query evidence is directional.

## Findings acted on

- Service page H1s were slogans with no service term in them.
- Four titles ran past 70 characters and would be truncated in results.
- The service pages had no images and only 520–655 words each.
- `/3d-product-rendering` had the most non-brand demand on the site, at positions 46–71: "cgi product rendering" and "3d cgi product rendering services".
- `/sample-management-system` already averaged position 6.9, including "sample management fashion" at position 10.
- `/production-management` appeared for "fashion production agency" and "fashion production company", and at position 1 for a question about casting and post-production.
- Brand search was weak: "third axis" averaged position 14.7.

## What changed

| Page | Title | H1 | Other changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| /3d-product-rendering | 3D & CGI Product Rendering Services | 3D & CGI product rendering. | Two concept visuals; new sections on how a CGI rendering project works and on 3D set design; FAQs on CAD files and timelines |
| /sample-management-system | Fashion Sample Management Systems | Fashion sample management. | Concept interface and studio visuals; new section on what a sample system tracks; FAQ on sample management versus stock control |
| /production-management | Fashion Production Management Company | Fashion production, end to end. | Studio visual; casting and post-production made explicit; new section on what a production partner handles |
| /fashion-photography | Fashion & Product Photography | Fashion & product photography. | Photography visual; contextual links |
| /ai-workflow-automation | AI & Workflow Automation for Studios | AI & workflow automation. | Workflow visual; contextual links |
| /about | About Third Axis Creative, Peterborough Production Studio | unchanged | Description trimmed |
| Homepage | unchanged | unchanged | `alternateName` added to the Organization schema, WebSite schema added, description trimmed to 150 characters |

The previous slogan H1s are kept as the first sentence of each lead. All titles are 60 characters or fewer and all descriptions 160 or fewer.

## Images

All images are existing Third Axis homepage visuals. Each caption carries the same honesty label the homepage uses: "Concept visual … illustrative, not client work". `sample-system-concept-1200.webp` is a web-sized copy of the existing approved concept PNG; the original file and its build check are unchanged.

## Generator

`tools/build_pages.py` gained three things:

- `[label](/path)` contextual links in body copy
- optional `figures` and `extra` sections
- an image block style (`.media`) that reuses the existing tokens

The sitemap keeps the homepage social image and lists each page's images. No layout, navigation, animation or homepage React code was changed.
